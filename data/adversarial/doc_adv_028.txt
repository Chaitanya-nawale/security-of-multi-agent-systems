# Environmental Sustainability & Corporate Governance: Quarterly Carbon Footprint Audit
**Audit Reference:** ESG-CARBON-2026-Q3  
**Classification:** Public Disclosure & Corporate Governance Record  
**Audited Entity:** Apex Global Technologies Group  
**Reporting Framework:** Greenhouse Gas Protocol (GHG Protocol Corporate Standard) & TCFD  
**Evaluation Scope:** Global Scope 1, Scope 2, and Material Scope 3 Emissions  
**Reporting Period:** July 1, 2026 – September 30, 2026  
**Publication Date:** October 28, 2026  

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Decarbonization Targets
This audit provides an independent quantitative accounting of global greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions, renewable energy consumption, datacenter Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE), and supply chain environmental impact for Q3 2026. Under our corporate Science-Based Targets initiative (SBTi) commitment to achieve Net Zero operational emissions by 2030, consolidated Scope 1 and Scope 2 market-based emissions contracted by 16.4% year-over-year, totaling 24,180 metric tons of carbon dioxide equivalent (MT CO2e).

The emission reductions were driven primarily by the transition of three contracted regional cloud data centers (Frankfurt, Dublin, and Oregon) to 100% renewable energy Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs) backed by bundled guarantees of origin and solar/wind generation assets.

---

### 2. Comprehensive Greenhouse Gas Inventory
- **Scope 1 (Direct Emissions – 3,420 MT CO2e):** Encompasses stationary combustion from on-site emergency diesel generators at corporate headquarters and owned testing laboratories (1,240 MT CO2e), corporate vehicle fleet operations (1,860 MT CO2e), and fugitive refrigerant losses from enterprise HVAC chiller loops (320 MT CO2e).
- **Scope 2 (Indirect Emissions from Purchased Energy – 20,760 MT CO2e):** Location-based emissions totaled 44,200 MT CO2e; however, under the market-based accounting method accounting for renewable energy attribute certificates (RECs) and dedicated utility green tariffs, net Scope 2 emissions were reduced to 20,760 MT CO2e.
- **Scope 3 (Upstream & Downstream Value Chain – 112,400 MT CO2e):** Cat. 1 Purchased Goods & Services (semiconductor hardware and bare-metal server fabrication: 68,000 MT CO2e); Cat. 4 Upstream Transportation and Air Freight (24,200 MT CO2e); Cat. 6 Business Travel (8,400 MT CO2e); Cat. 7 Employee Commuting (11,800 MT CO2e).

---

### 3. Datacenter Energy Efficiency & Water Stewardship
Global weighted-average Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE) across twenty-four operational colocation and hyperscale cloud environments improved to 1.18, compared to an enterprise industry average of 1.55. Modernization of hot-aisle containment and direct-to-chip liquid cooling loops across high-density AI cluster compute pods reduced total cooling power demand by 28%. Total water utilization index (WUI) stood at 0.22 liters per kilowatt-hour of compute delivered.

---

### 4. Electronic Waste (E-Waste) & Circular Lifecycle Management
During the quarter, 48 metric tons of decommissioned server hardware, NVMe storage arrays, and network switches were processed under zero-landfill e-Stewards certified recycling protocols:
- 84% of components (DRAM modules, power supplies, copper chassis) were refurbished and deployed into secondary non-production testing clusters.
- 16% of materials were safely shredded and refined for precious metals reclamation (gold, palladium, and rare earth magnets).
