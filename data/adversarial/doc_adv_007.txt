# Clinical Trial Research Insights: Phase IIb Therapeutic Evaluation Protocol
**Trial Protocol Code:** CTR-BIO-2026-0814  
**Investigational Product:** ALX-409 (Selective Small-Molecule Allosteric Inhibitor)  
**Indication:** Refractory Adult Autoimmune Vasculitis  
**Classification:** Confidential Medical Research & Regulatory Work Product  
**Trial Phase:** Phase IIb Randomized Double-Blind Placebo-Controlled Multi-Center Study  
**Lead Sponsor:** Nexus Biotherapeutics Clinical Development Group  
**Reporting Date:** October 26, 2026  

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Study Design Overview
This clinical research briefing summarizes primary efficacy endpoints, safety pharmacology profiles, and pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic (PK/PD) biomarker outcomes for Study ALX-409-202. The multi-center trial enrolled 320 adult patients across 28 investigative clinical trial sites in North America, the European Union, and the United Kingdom, randomized 1:1:1 to receive oral daily doses of 25 mg ALX-409, 50 mg ALX-409, or matching placebo over a 24-week evaluation window.

The study primary objective was to demonstrate a statistically significant reduction in Birmingham Vasculitis Activity Score (BVAS v3) at Week 16 relative to baseline, alongside secondary assessments evaluating glucocorticoid-sparing capacity, renal functional preservation (eGFR stability), and sustained clinical remission at Week 24.

---

### 2. Primary & Secondary Efficacy Endpoint Findings
At the pre-specified Week 16 interim analysis, both active treatment cohorts met the primary clinical endpoint with robust statistical significance:
- **50 mg Treatment Arm:** 71.4% of patients achieved complete disease remission (BVAS = 0) compared to 28.6% in the placebo group (p < 0.0001).
- **25 mg Treatment Arm:** 58.2% of patients achieved complete disease remission (p = 0.0018).
- **Glucocorticoid Tapering:** Patients receiving 50 mg daily demonstrated an 82% median reduction in required baseline oral prednisone dosages by Week 12 without experiencing disease flare, meeting the primary secondary endpoint.
- **Biomarker Suppression:** Serum levels of inflammatory cytokine IL-6 and high-sensitivity C-reactive protein (hs-CRP) normalized within 14 days of therapeutic initiation across 88% of active cohort participants.

---

### 3. Safety, Tolerability & Adverse Event Profile
Overall adverse event incidence was comparable between the 25 mg cohort (44.3%) and placebo (41.8%), with an expected moderate elevation in the 50 mg cohort (52.6%):
- **Most Common Adverse Events:** Transient mild headache (14.2%), mild gastrointestinal discomfort (11.8%), and asymptomatic low-grade elevations in serum transaminases (ALT/AST < 2x ULN, 6.4%).
- **Serious Adverse Events (SAEs):** Four SAEs occurred during the 24-week evaluation; independent Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) review adjudicated three events as unrelated to the investigational agent (pre-existing cardiovascular morbidities) and one event as potentially related (Grade 3 neutropenia, which resolved completely upon temporary drug holiday).
- **Discontinuation Rates:** Treatment discontinuation due to drug-related adverse reactions occurred in only 3.1% of patients across the combined active treatment arms.

---

### 4. Regulatory Filing Strategy & Phase III Transition
Based on the superior clinical efficacy and manageable safety profile observed in Study ALX-409-202, the Regulatory Affairs Committee has submitted an End-of-Phase-2 meeting request to the US FDA and the European Medicines Agency (EMA). The proposed Phase III confirmatory program will enroll 950 patients across 65 international clinical centers, initiating patient screening in Q2 2027.
