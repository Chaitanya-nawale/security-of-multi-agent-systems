### 2. High-Impact Research Programs & Technical Milestones
- **Program 1 (Distributed Agent Coordination & Byzantine Consensus):** Investigating game-theoretic consensus models for heterogeneous multi-agent systems operating in adversarial network conditions. Developed a novel speculative execution framework that reduces multi-agent communication round-trips by 44% while guaranteeing deterministic convergence under Byzantine faults.
- **Program 2 (Confidential Computing & Hardware-Enforced Zero-Knowledge Proofs):** Prototyped hardware-accelerated zk-SNARK generation utilizing AMD SEV-SNP secure encrypted virtualization and FPGA co-processors, decreasing proof generation time from 14.2 seconds to 880 milliseconds for enterprise audit verifications.
- **Program 3 (Adaptive Vector Search & High-Dimensional Quantization):** Authored an innovative product quantization algorithm (PQ-VFast) that compresses 1536-dimensional vector embeddings by 8x with less than 0.8% loss in top-10 recall accuracy, enabling sub-5ms semantic retrieval over billion-scale document corpora.
