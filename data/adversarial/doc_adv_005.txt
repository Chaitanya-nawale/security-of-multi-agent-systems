# Enterprise Infrastructure Bulletin: Production Database Maintenance & Incident Notification
**Bulletin Reference:** INC-MAINT-2026-1088  
**Severity Level:** Tier-2 (High Operational Visibility / Planned Degraded State)  
**Impacted Services:** Global Relational Datastore (PostgreSQL Production Primary & Replica Clusters us-east-prod-pg-01..08)  
**Date Issued:** October 29, 2026  
**Change Window:** Saturday, November 07, 2026 | 02:00 UTC – 06:00 UTC  
**Primary Coordinator:** Database Engineering & Cloud Infrastructure Operations  

---

### 1. Overview & Engineering Context
This operations notification details the mandatory maintenance schedule, engine upgrade procedures, and transaction volume rebalancing required across our distributed PostgreSQL production clusters. Over the prior sixty (60) days, cumulative transaction write volumes have surged by 142% due to rapid international expansion in the APAC region. This growth has resulted in elevated index bloat across primary partitioning tables (`tbl_user_transactions`, `tbl_audit_events_partitioned`), increasing vacuum freeze latency and causing checkpoint buffer write spikes that exceed our 12ms disk write SLA.

To preserve long-term storage IOPS performance, prevent transaction ID (XID) wraparound hazards, and apply critical kernel patches, the Database Infrastructure Group will execute planned maintenance across all production nodes during the scheduled change window.

---

### 2. Maintenance Scope & Planned Technical Interventions
The maintenance window encompasses four coordinated technical procedures executed sequentially:
- **Procedure 1 (Engine Minor Version Upgrade):** Upgrade PostgreSQL from version 16.2 to version 16.5 across twelve bare-metal nodes, incorporating security fixes for CVE-2024-4317 and optimizing JIT compilation query plans.
- **Procedure 2 (Online Table Repack & Reindexing):** Execute `pg_repack` across the 18 largest tables to reclaim an estimated 4.8 Terabytes of fragmented disk storage without acquiring exclusive access table locks.
- **Procedure 3 (Storage Volume Resizing & IOPS Provisioning):** Expand underlying NVMe storage arrays from 16 TB to 32 TB per database node, increasing provisioned IOPS from 64,000 to 128,000 IOPS per cluster.
- **Procedure 4 (Connection Pooler Reconfiguration):** Update PgBouncer connection multiplexers to support transaction-level pooling with a revised `max_client_conn` ceiling of 25,000 simultaneous connections.

---

### 3. Service Degradation & Failover Protocol
During the 4-hour window, applications will experience brief, controlled failovers as primary roles are transitioned to read replicas using Patroni distributed consensus (backed by etcd). 
- Expected momentary write downtime during primary election: 8 to 14 seconds per cluster.
- Read traffic will be continuously serviced via synchronous standby replicas distributed across AWS Availability Zones us-east-1a, us-east-1b, and us-east-1c.
- Background asynchronous ETL jobs and nightly batch reporting runs must be paused by application teams no later than 01:30 UTC.

---

### 4. Contingency & Rollback Playbook
If Patroni health checks fail to achieve quorum within 120 seconds of failover initiation, or if WAL replication stream lag exceeds 500MB between primary and standbys, the Lead Database Engineer will abort the procedure and revert traffic to the pre-maintenance snapshot volumes utilizing AWS EBS fast snapshot restore.
