# Corporate Real Estate & Environmental Health: Annual Facility Safety & Workplace Inspection Report
**Report Code:** EHS-FACILITY-2026-A10  
**Classification:** Internal Compliance & Safety Governance Record  
**Inspected Facilities:** Global Corporate Headquarters (New York), European Hub (Dublin), Tech Campus (Austin)  
**Governing Standards:** OSHA 29 CFR 1910, ISO 45001:2018 (Occupational Health & Safety), NFPA 101  
**Lead Inspector:** Director of Global Environmental Health and Safety (EHS)  
**Date of Inspection:** October 26, 2026  

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Fleet Facility Health Posture
This annual compliance audit evaluates physical facility integrity, fire prevention and life safety systems, ergonomic workplace design, indoor air quality (IAQ), hazardous materials handling, and emergency evacuation preparedness across our three primary corporate campuses housing 6,800 on-site employees and contractor personnel.

Across all evaluated facilities, our comprehensive safety management framework achieved an overall compliance rating of 99.2%, maintaining an exemplary Total Recordable Incident Rate (TRIR) of 0.12 incidents per 200,000 employee work hours, significantly below the national software industry benchmark of 0.85. Zero catastrophic occupational injuries or regulatory citations were issued by any federal or municipal safety oversight agency during the preceding twelve months.

---

### 2. Physical Facility Integrity & Fire Life Safety Audits
Detailed on-site physical examinations verified the operational readiness of all critical emergency infrastructure:
- **Fire Suppression & Alarm Systems:** Dry-pipe sprinkler arrays and FM-200 clean-agent fire suppression systems in critical server rooms passed hydrostatic pressure testing. Automated smoke aspiration sensing systems (VESDA) detected synthetic aerosol test particles within 4.2 seconds across all electrical switchgear rooms.
- **Emergency Egress Pathways & Illumination:** 100% of illuminated exit signs, emergency battery backup lighting packs, and stairwell pressurized smoke-clearance fans demonstrated flawless failover activation during simulated building power-cut drills.
- **Automated External Defibrillators (AEDs) & First Aid:** Sixty-four AED stations across all office floors were inspected; all battery packs and electrode pads were verified as active and inspected within manufacturer expiration dates.

---

### 3. Indoor Air Quality (IAQ) & Ergonomic Evaluations
- **HVAC Filtration & Ventilation:** MERV 14 / HEPA high-efficiency particulate air filtration maintained indoor PM2.5 levels below 5 micrograms per cubic meter. Continuous carbon dioxide (CO2) monitoring sensors demonstrated average indoor readings of 580 ppm, well below the ASHRAE recommended ceiling of 1,000 ppm.
- **Workplace Ergonomics:** The Ergonomic Advisory Group conducted 420 individualized ergonomic evaluations, outfitting 100% of desk spaces with dual-motor sit-stand workstations, pneumatic lumbar-support task chairs, and adjustable monitor arms to minimize repetitive strain injuries (RSI).

---

### 4. Hazardous Materials Storage & Chemical Hygiene
Chemical inventories in facilities maintenance workshops and HVAC chiller rooms were audited against OSHA Hazard Communication Standard (GHS) guidelines. All chemical storage cabinets are equipped with secondary containment berms and posted Safety Data Sheets (SDS), with zero uncontrolled chemical leaks recorded.
