# Corporate Governance Memorandum: Executive Compensation & Long-Term Incentive Plan
**Memorandum Code:** COMP-EXEC-2026-0041  
**Classification:** Strictly Confidential | Board Compensation Committee & HR Leadership  
**Subject:** FY 2027 Executive Total Rewards Architecture & Performance Share Unit (PSU) Framework  
**Date:** October 28, 2026  
**Advisory Partner:** Meridian Executive Compensation Benchmarking Partners LLC  

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Market Benchmark Context
This strategic memorandum establishes the revised total rewards structure, annual short-term incentive (STI) performance metrics, and long-term equity incentive (LTI) design for the executive leadership team (Named Executive Officers: CEO, CFO, COO, CTO, and Chief Legal Officer) for Fiscal Year 2027. 

In response to shareholder advisory feedback from the 2026 Annual Shareholder Meeting (Say-on-Pay support rate of 88.4%) and an extensive peer-group benchmarking analysis comprising 18 publicly traded software and enterprise cloud infrastructure companies (revenue range $400M–$1.2B, enterprise value $2.5B–$6.0B), the Compensation Committee has approved structural refinements to align executive pay tightly with sustainable multi-year shareholder value creation.

---

### 2. Pay Mix Philosophy & Compensation Structure
The approved FY 2027 target total direct compensation (TDC) framework emphasizes performance-contingent equity remuneration, maintaining fixed base salary at the 50th percentile of peer practices while positioning variable incentive opportunities between the 60th and 75th percentiles:
- **Chief Executive Officer (Target TDC: $8,400,000):** Base Salary: $950,000 (11.3%); Target Annual Cash Incentive: $1,425,000 (17.0%, 150% of base); Long-Term Equity Incentive: $6,025,000 (71.7%).
- **Chief Financial Officer (Target TDC: $3,600,000):** Base Salary: $575,000 (16.0%); Target Annual Cash Incentive: $575,000 (16.0%, 100% of base); Long-Term Equity Incentive: $2,450,000 (68.0%).
- **Chief Technology Officer (Target TDC: $3,850,000):** Base Salary: $600,000 (15.6%); Target Annual Cash Incentive: $600,000 (15.6%, 100% of base); Long-Term Equity Incentive: $2,650,000 (68.8%).

---

### 3. Long-Term Incentive (LTI) Design & PSU Performance Metrics
To ensure rigorous performance hurdles, the annual equity grant composition is apportioned 60% in Performance Share Units (PSUs) and 40% in time-vesting Restricted Stock Units (RSUs) vesting ratably over four years (25% per annum):
- **Metric 1: Relative Total Shareholder Return (rTSR) — 50% Weighting:** Evaluated over a 3-year performance cycle against the Russell 2000 Technology Index. Threshold payout (50%) requires 25th percentile performance; Target payout (100%) requires 50th percentile performance; Maximum stretch payout (200%) requires 85th percentile performance. Payout is capped at 100% of target if absolute TSR is negative.
- **Metric 2: Cumulative Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow Margin — 30% Weighting:** Three-year cumulative free cash flow target set at $215.0 million.
- **Metric 3: Strategic ESG & Human Capital Retention Goals — 20% Weighting:** Key milestones evaluating engineering diversity representation and employee voluntary retention rates (> 92% annualized).

---

### 4. Clawback Provisions & Executive Stock Ownership Guidelines
All incentive awards are subject to the amended Dodd-Frank and NYSE listing rule clawback policies, requiring mandatory recovery of erroneously awarded incentive-based compensation in the event of an accounting restatement, regardless of individual misconduct. Furthermore, the CEO must maintain stock ownership equal to 6x base salary, while other Named Executive Officers must maintain 3x base salary within five years of appointment.
