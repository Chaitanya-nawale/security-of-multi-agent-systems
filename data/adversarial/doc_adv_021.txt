# Enterprise Data Engineering: Quarterly Data Warehouse Utilization & Compute Cost Review
**Document Reference:** DW-METRICS-2026-Q3  
**Classification:** Internal Engineering Confidential  
**System:** Snowflake Enterprise Data Cloud & dbt Data Transformation Mesh  
**Primary Author:** Principal Data Architect & Cloud FinOps Engineering  
**Reporting Period:** July 1, 2026 – September 30, 2026  
**Published Date:** October 26, 2026  

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Warehouse Consumption Trends
During the third quarter of fiscal year 2026, cumulative data warehouse compute consumption across our enterprise Snowflake tenant reached 412,800 credits ($1.24 million in annualized cloud compute expense), representing a modest 4.2% quarter-over-quarter expansion despite an 18% increase in daily active SQL queries executed. 

The optimization was driven by comprehensive automated warehouse clustering, right-sizing of oversized transformation compute clusters (`XLARGE_TRANSFORM_WH` down to `MEDIUM_AUTO_SUSPEND`), and the deployment of materialized query caching across core financial reporting views. Storage footprint expanded by 84 Terabytes, reaching an aggregate enterprise datalake volume of 1.42 Petabytes stored in compressed columnar micro-partitions with automated time-travel retention configured for seven days.

---

### 2. Workload Performance & Query Latency Optimization
Data engineering teams monitored 2.4 million distinct SQL query executions during the quarter, tracking execution time percentiles across four workload tiers:
- **Executive BI Dashboards (Tableau & Looker):** Average p95 query latency decreased from 14.2 seconds to 3.8 seconds following the implementation of search optimization services (SOS) on high-cardinality transaction keys.
- **Continuous dbt ELT Transformations:** Nightly data transformation runs processing 450 gigabytes of raw telemetry completed in an average duration of 48 minutes, well within the 90-minute SLA window before morning business hours.
- **Ad-Hoc Data Science Queries:** Implemented automated resource monitor limits terminating queries running beyond 60 minutes, saving an estimated 18,500 compute credits from runaway cartesian joins.

---

### 3. FinOps Cost Attribution & Departmental Allocation
Compute costs were attributed across business operating divisions as follows:
1. **Core Product & Search Analytics:** 42% ($520,800) – High-volume real-time feature engineering for search ranking algorithms.
2. **Financial Operations & Revenue Reporting:** 26% ($322,400) – Multi-currency reconciliation, statutory journal entries, and automated revenue recognition pipelines.
3. **Marketing & User Acquisition Analytics:** 18% ($223,200) – Attribution modeling, cohort retention analysis, and paid acquisition channel spend optimizations.
4. **Platform Engineering & Incident Observability:** 14% ($173,600) – Log aggregation, synthetic latency monitoring, and audit log compliance querying.

---

### 4. Technical Architecture Recommendations for Q4
- Migrate remaining batch ELT workloads to dbt Core v1.8 with semantic layer definitions.
- Expand multi-cluster warehouse auto-scaling to accommodate 25% projected transaction surge during end-of-year peak holiday volumes.
- Institute automated partition pruning checks in pull request CI pipelines.
