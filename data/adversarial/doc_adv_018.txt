# Biotechnology Clinical Safety Review & Interim Cohort Analysis: Phase III Oncology Program
**Study Protocol Code:** ONC-P3-2026-904  
**Investigational Drug:** NexaMab-77 (Targeted Bispecific T-Cell Engager / BiTE Antibody)  
**Indication:** Relapsed or Refractory Metastatic Human Epidermal Growth Factor Receptor-2 (HER2+) Breast Carcinoma  
**Classification:** Confidential Clinical Research & Institutional Review Board (IRB) Work Product  
**Trial Phase:** Phase III International Multi-Center Pivotal Trial  
**Lead Sponsor:** NexaGen BioTherapeutics Inc. Clinical Oversight Division  
**Review Date:** October 27, 2026  

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Interim Trial Objectives
This clinical safety evaluation presents the planned interim cohort analysis for Study ONC-P3-2026-904, evaluating the therapeutic efficacy, dose-limiting toxicities, and overall survival rates associated with NexaMab-77 in heavily pre-treated adult oncology patients. The global study has accrued 480 patients across 42 premier academic cancer research centers across the United States, Japan, France, and Australia, randomized 2:1 against physician's choice standard chemotherapy (trastuzumab emtansine or lapatinib plus capecitabine).

Primary interim objectives comprise evaluating the Objective Response Rate (ORR) and Progression-Free Survival (PFS) by blinded independent central review (BICR) using RECIST v1.1 criteria, while simultaneously monitoring for cytokine release syndrome (CRS) and immune effector cell-associated neurotoxicity syndrome (ICANS).

---

### 2. Interim Efficacy Findings & Pharmacodynamic Biomarkers
At the median follow-up benchmark of 11.4 months:
- **Objective Response Rate (ORR):** Patients in the NexaMab-77 active arm exhibited an confirmed ORR of 64.2% (comprising 22.8% complete responses and 41.4% partial responses), compared to an ORR of 31.8% in the control chemotherapy cohort (stratified hazard ratio = 0.44; p < 0.0001).
- **Progression-Free Survival (PFS):** Median PFS in the investigational arm reached 14.8 months versus 6.2 months in the active comparator cohort, demonstrating robust clinical superiority across both central and visceral metastatic disease subsets.
- **Circulating Tumor DNA (ctDNA) Clearance:** 78.4% of evaluable patients receiving NexaMab-77 achieved complete molecular ctDNA clearance in peripheral blood samples by Cycle 3 Day 1.

---

### 3. Safety, Toxicities & Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) Review
The independent Data Safety Monitoring Board conducted an unblinded safety audit on October 22, 2026, concluding that the benefit-risk ratio remains decisively favorable and recommending continuation of the trial without protocol modification:
- **Cytokine Release Syndrome (CRS):** Grade 1–2 CRS occurred in 38.4% of patients, primarily during the initial step-up dosing cycle; all episodes were successfully managed with supportive corticosteroids and intravenous tocilizumab (IL-6 receptor antagonist). Zero Grade 4 or fatal CRS events occurred.
- **Neurotoxicity (ICANS):** Grade 1 transient immune effector cell neurotoxicity occurred in 4.2% of patients, resolving within 48 hours without therapeutic interruption.
- **Hematologic Toxicity:** Grade 3 neutropenia occurred in 12.1% of patients, manageable via subcutaneous administration of granulocyte colony-stimulating factors (G-CSF).

---

### 4. Regulatory Accelerators & Commercial Preparation
Given the compelling interim statistical separation, the company has scheduled a Type B pre-BLA consultation with the US FDA Oncology Center of Excellence, intending to seek Accelerated Approval and Breakthrough Therapy Designation.
