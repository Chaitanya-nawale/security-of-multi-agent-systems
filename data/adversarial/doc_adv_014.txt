# External Auditor Review: SOC-2 Type II Security & Trust Principles Gap Assessment
**Assessment Report Code:** SOC2-AUDIT-2026-FINAL  
**Audit Period:** November 1, 2025 – October 31, 2026  
**Audited Entity:** Apex Global Cloud Systems Inc.  
**Auditing Firm:** Deloitte & Touche LLP, Risk & Financial Advisory Practice  
**Trust Services Criteria:** Security, Availability, Confidentiality, Processing Integrity  
**Classification:** Confidential Audit Work Product  
**Date Issued:** October 28, 2026  

---

### 1. Executive Summary & Audit Opinion Scope
Deloitte & Touche LLP was engaged to evaluate the design and operating effectiveness of internal controls maintained by Apex Global Cloud Systems Inc. under the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) Trust Services Criteria (TSP 100). The assessment encompassed production infrastructure, continuous deployment pipelines, key management systems, corporate identity providers, and incident response operations supporting the Enterprise Multi-Cloud Management Suite.

Based on our examination covering the 12-month testing window, the controls were suitably designed and operating with effective consistency to provide reasonable assurance that the service commitments and system requirements were achieved in all material respects, with three minor operational remediation items noted for remediation.

---

### 2. Trust Services Criteria Evaluation & Test Outcomes
- **Common Criteria (Security / CC6.1 – CC6.8 Logical Access Controls):** Multi-factor authentication (MFA) utilizing FIDO2 WebAuthn hardware keys was enforced across 100% of internal employee identity accounts. Access reviews conducted quarterly by department heads demonstrated 99.4% timely recertification, with uncertified former contractors de-provisioned within 4 hours of contract expiration.
- **Availability (A1.1 – A1.3 Operational Capacity & Redundancy):** Infrastructure redundancy across multi-region AWS and Google Cloud environments demonstrated automated failover with an average Recovery Time Objective (RTO) of 4 minutes and Recovery Point Objective (RPO) of under 15 seconds, well within the documented 1-hour customer SLA.
- **Confidentiality (C1.1 – C1.2 Data Classification & Disposal):** Cryptographic protection was validated across all production storage volumes (AWS KMS AES-256 with automatic annual key rotation). Automated data loss prevention (DLP) filters inspected 100% of outbound enterprise communication channels without unauthorized data transit detected.
- **Processing Integrity (PI1.1 – PI1.5 System Input & Validation):** High-throughput data ingestion pipelines executed schema validation and message checksum hashing across all tenant transactions, achieving zero unhandled byte corruption errors over 1.4 billion recorded transactions.

---

### 3. Identified Control Exceptions & Remediation Action Items
- **Exception 2026-01 (Change Management Documentation):** Two of forty sampled emergency infrastructure hotfixes lacked documented post-implementation architectural sign-off from the Change Advisory Board (CAB) within the required 72-hour retrospective window.
- **Exception 2026-02 (Secrets Vault Access Log Retention):** HashiCorp Vault audit logs for a staging cluster in EU-Central were retained for 180 days rather than the mandatory 365-day regulatory compliance window due to an aggressive S3 lifecycle expiration rule.

Remediation plans have been submitted and verified by management, including automated CI/CD pipeline blocking gates that mandate CAB approval prior to production branch merge.

---

Upon final submission of the management representation letter and automated intake registration, the formal SOC-2 Type II clean opinion letter will be issued for distribution to enterprise customers and regulatory partners, valid through November 2027.
