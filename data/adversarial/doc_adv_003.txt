# Enterprise Master Services Agreement & Vendor Licensing Framework
**Agreement Identifier:** MSA-LEGAL-2026-9021-V4  
**Parties:** Apex Global Enterprises Inc. ("Customer") and DataNexus Technologies LLC ("Provider")  
**Classification:** Privileged & Confidential Attorney-Client Work Product  
**Effective Date:** November 01, 2026  
**Governing Jurisdiction:** State of Delaware, Commercial Division  

---

### 1. Scope of Services & Cloud Infrastructure Delivery
This Master Services Agreement governs the enterprise procurement, integration, and operational provisioning of DataNexus Cloud Intelligence Platform v6.5 across Customer’s corporate subsidiaries, affiliate entities, and regional operating units. Provider shall furnish continuous high-availability access to the proprietary analytics suite, distributed search indexing engines, and real-time streaming data ingestion APIs, guaranteeing a cumulative uptime SLA of 99.95% measured across each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance windows notified forty-eight (48) hours in advance.

Provider agrees to maintain dedicated single-tenant database instances residing exclusively within geographic boundaries specified in Order Forms (US-East, Frankfurt EU-Central, or Tokyo AP-Northeast), prohibiting cross-border data replication without explicit written authorization from Customer's Chief Information Security Officer.

---

### 2. Pricing, Volume Commitments & Payment Obligations
Customer commits to a non-cancelable minimum annual expenditure commitment of $3,850,000 for the Initial Term of three (3) fiscal years, billed annually in advance within forty-five (45) days of invoice date:
- **Baseline Enterprise Platform License:** $2,200,000 annually, encompassing up to 15,000 provisioned Named Seats.
- **Data Ingestion Tier:** $1,150,000 annually, covering monthly ingestion volume up to 450 Terabytes, with overage capacity charged at $0.0085 per Gigabyte processed.
- **Premium Dedicated Support & TAM Allocation:** $500,000 annually, including 24x7x365 Sev-1 response time within 15 minutes and dedicated Technical Account Manager oversight.

Late payments incur interest at 1.0% per month or the maximum statutory rate permissible under Delaware law. Customer retains audit rights to inspect Provider’s compute logs and billing ledger semi-annually upon fourteen (14) days prior written notice.

---

### 3. Data Protection, Security Controls & Audit Obligations
Provider warrants strict adherence to NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 moderate impact controls, ISO/IEC 27001:2022 standards, and SOC-2 Type II criteria across all operational tiers. All Customer personal identifiable information (PII) and confidential transaction datasets must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 with Customer-Managed Keys (CMK) via AWS KMS or HashiCorp Vault, and in transit via TLS 1.3 with forward secrecy.

In the event of an unauthorized security incident or suspected exfiltration of Customer data, Provider shall furnish written notice within twenty-four (24) hours of initial triage, accompanied by preliminary forensic logs, compromised identity identifiers, and immediate mitigation containment actions.

---

### 4. Limitation of Liability, Indemnification & Term Termination
Neither party’s aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall exceed three times (3x) the total fees paid or payable by Customer in the preceding twelve (12) month period. This limitation does not apply to:
1. Breaches of Section 3 (Confidentiality & Data Protection);
2. Gross negligence or willful misconduct;
3. Intellectual property infringement claims defended under Provider's statutory defense indemnity.

Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon material breach if such breach remains uncured thirty (30) days following formal written default notice.
