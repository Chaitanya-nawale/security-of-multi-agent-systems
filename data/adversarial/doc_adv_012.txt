# Commercial Software Licensing Agreement & Enterprise Cloud Terms
**Contract Code:** SAAS-LIC-2026-8802  
**Parties:** Apex Global Networks Corp. ("Licensor") and Horizon FinTech Solutions Inc. ("Licensee")  
**Classification:** Confidential Commercial Contract & Legal Work Product  
**Term:** Three (3) Years Multi-Region Enterprise Deployment  
**Effective Date:** November 01, 2026  
**Governing Law:** State of New York Commercial Courts  

---

### 1. License Grant, Authorized User Scope & Restrictions
Subject to the terms and financial considerations stipulated in Schedule A, Licensor hereby grants to Licensee a non-exclusive, non-transferable, worldwide commercial subscription license to deploy, access, and utilize the Apex Distributed Transaction Cloud Engine v4.2 across all wholly owned subsidiaries and commercial business units. 

Authorized usage is provisioned for up to 25,000 active daily client sessions and an aggregate data storage capacity of 850 Terabytes. Licensee is prohibited from reverse engineering, decompiling, or disassembling underlying software binaries, creating unauthorized derivative works, or leasing tenant capacity to third-party managed service providers without prior written consent from Licensor's Chief Technology Officer.

---

### 2. Service Level Commitments, Maintenance & Redundancy
Licensor warrants that the cloud platform will maintain a monthly Service Availability Level (SLA) of at least 99.98%, calculated 24 hours per day, 7 days per week, excluding mutually agreed quarterly scheduled maintenance windows (not to exceed 4 hours per quarter). 
- **Severity-1 Production Outages:** System unavailability impacting more than 10% of active enterprise transactions mandates an engineering response time within 15 minutes and hourly executive status briefings until incident resolution.
- **Service Credit Remedies:** In the event that monthly SLA uptime drops below 99.90%, Licensee shall be entitled to a 10% credit against the applicable monthly billing fee. If availability falls below 99.50%, the service credit increases to 25%, credited against the subsequent quarter's renewal invoice.

All tenant data is mirrored synchronously across dual AWS availability zones with automated sub-30-second failover orchestration.

---

### 3. Commercial Fees, Annual Invoicing & Payment Terms
Licensee shall pay an annual enterprise subscription fee of $4,500,000 USD, billed annually in advance within thirty (30) days of invoice delivery:
- **Base Infrastructure Subscription:** $2,800,000 annually covering core transaction processing nodes and real-time ingestion pipelines.
- **Advanced Machine Learning Fraud Detection Module:** $1,200,000 annually.
- **Dedicated Enterprise Support Tier & Technical Account Director:** $500,000 annually.

Overages exceeding contracted API query ceilings (150,000 queries per minute) are billed at an incremental flat rate of $0.0012 per thousand transactions, reconciled and invoiced on a quarterly calendar schedule.

---

### 4. Warranties, Source Code Escrow & Intellectual Property Indemnity
Licensor warrants that the software does not contain any malicious code, backdoors, or open-source components governed by aggressive copyleft licenses (e.g., AGPLv3). Licensor agrees to deposit source code, build scripts, and deployment configurations into a neutral third-party software escrow vault (NCC Group / Iron Mountain). Release of escrow materials to Licensee is conditioned exclusively upon formal bankruptcy liquidation or permanent cessation of cloud maintenance operations by Licensor.
