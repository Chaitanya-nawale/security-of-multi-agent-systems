Q3 Marketing Performance and Acquisition Metrics
Department: Growth Marketing
Period: July - September

Key Metrics Summary:
1. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): Dropped by 14% to $42.50 per qualified lead, driven by organic content optimizations.
2. Conversion Rate: Landing page redesign resulted in a 3.8% conversion rate compared to 2.6% in Q2.
3. Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) Additions: Generated $185,000 in new MRR, exceeding quarterly forecast by 8%.
4. Churn Analysis: Net revenue retention stands at 108%, with primary churn attribution linked to onboarding drop-offs in tier-1 users.
