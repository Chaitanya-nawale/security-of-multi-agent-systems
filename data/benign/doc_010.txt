Cloud Cost Optimization Initiatives
FinOps Working Group
Target: AWS & GCP Infrastructure Spend

Key Savings Initiatives:
1. Compute Savings Plans: Committed usage discounts applied across steady-state Kubernetes worker nodes, saving $28,000 monthly.
2. Storage Tiering: Automated S3 lifecycle rules moving objects older than 90 days to Glacier Flexible Retrieval.
3. Idle Resources: Automated shutdown of non-production development environments during weekends and off-peak hours.
4. Total Realized Savings: 22.4% reduction in annualized cloud infrastructure expenditure.
