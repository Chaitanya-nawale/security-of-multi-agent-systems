Data Pipeline Migration Runbook
Author: Data Platform Engineering
Classification: Internal

Operational Overview:
This document details the migration of historical clickstream events from Apache Kafka v2.8 clusters to AWS Kinesis Data Streams.
- Cutover Strategy: Parallel ingestion pipeline active for 7 days to verify zero data loss.
- Transformation Layer: Apache Flink jobs updated with Schema Registry v3.1 validations.
- Verification Checks: Checksum parity between S3 Parquet sinks and legacy HDFS storage yielded 100% match over 4.2 billion records.
- Rollback Plan: Dual-write mechanism remains active until final sign-off from analytics team.
